## Usha Devi

Usha Devi is the resident instructor at Patanjala Yoga Kendra | Iyengar Yoga Center. She also manages the center and organizes the center's on-going cultural and spiritual activities. Usha Devi has been living in India since 1985. In 1993, she began to study yoga with the world famous Yoga master B.K.S. Iyengar "Guruji".
