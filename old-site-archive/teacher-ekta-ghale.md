# Ekta Ghale

> Original URL: https://lightslategrey-finch-566548.hostingersite.com/yoga-studio/teachers/ekta-ghale-2/

Ekta Ghale started her training in Iyengar Yoga with Usha Devi in 2011 after she met Guru ji BKS Iyengar on his visit to the Center. Every summer she also studies and practices at Ramamani Iyengar Memorial Yoga Institute, Pune, for a few months.

At the center, Ekta has been teaching the daily classes for the beginners since 2015 as well as weekly classes for children and college students. For the past two years, she has also been teaching regular online classes to students from around the globe. Students love her classes especially because she is a very dedicated and caring teacher who accommodates everyone's needs and is mindful of a student's problems, if any.
